Privacy policy

Project Backup Utility is a personal-use tool operated by its account owner. This policy describes the backup utility and this information website, rather than any application whose files the owner chooses to back up.

Information the utility handles

The utility reads the local files selected for backup, which may include source files, configuration, private attachments and a stopped database. The owner determines the contents. Archives are encrypted locally before transfer to Google Drive.

The utility stores OAuth client configuration and refresh tokens locally to authenticate to Google. It uses the Google Drive API to create, read, check and manage files created or explicitly opened through this utility, and to read available-storage information. It does not request unrestricted access to unrelated Drive files.

Purpose and sharing

Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restoration functions requested by the owner. The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. The utility uploads encrypted backup files to the owner’s Google Drive; it does not publish them through this website.

Google processes stored encrypted files and service metadata under its own terms and privacy policy. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary connection and request logs under GitHub’s privacy policy. The website adds no analytics, advertising trackers, upload forms or application sign-in.

Security and retention

Snapshots use AES-256-GCM encryption. The encryption key is kept separately from cloud archives. Local credentials require private, owner-controlled storage. These controls reduce exposure but do not eliminate every risk; the owner must protect the computer, Google account and recovery key.

Under the current owner-approved policy, the newest three local snapshots are retained. An older generated local snapshot is removed only after its encrypted cloud copy is checked. Dated cloud snapshots are retained until the owner deletes them. A failed verification preserves the local copy.

Owner choices and deletion

The owner can stop scheduled backups, delete archives from their Drive folder, remove the local configuration, or revoke the utility’s access in their Google Account. Revoking access stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete existing Drive archives. The owner controls disposal of local copies and recovery keys.

Contact

Contact the operator through the project’s public issue tracker. Share no passwords, tokens, identity documents, backup files or other private information there.
